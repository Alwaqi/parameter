# Parameter Brand Reference

## Overview

Parameter uses a technical editorial system built around a dark gridded canvas, oversized sans-serif statements, and modular product cards. Sections alternate between near-black and cool white while preserving the same strict alignment and generous spacing. Friendly 3D robot imagery softens the otherwise precise systems language. Electric blue is reserved for emphasis, interactive states, and measurable outcomes.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#0E0F13` — the main dark canvas.
- **Raised Dark Surface**: `#1A1C22` — product cards and interface panels.
- **Primary Content**: `#F6F7F9` — headlines and high-priority copy on dark.
- **Light Surface**: `#FFFFFF` — service and tool sections.
- **Light-Surface Ink**: `#14161B` — headlines and controls on white.
- **Muted Content**: `#9EA1A9` — descriptions and secondary labels.
- **Brand Accent**: `#2D6BF5` — key words, active controls, and CTA emphasis.
- **Accent Highlight**: `#6FA0FF` — supporting data and glow.
- **Success**: `#00BA76` — live states and positive indicators.

## Typography

- **Display**: General Sans 600. Hero copy at 120px with tight `-2.4px` tracking and 1.0 line-height; section headlines at 56–68px.
- **Body**: Satoshi 400–500. Supporting copy at 19–24px with relaxed line-height, optically lighter on dark surfaces.
- **Technical Voice**: JetBrains Mono 500–600. Uppercase section indices, status labels, and data readouts with wide tracking.
- Keep major statements sentence case; reserve uppercase for short system labels.

## Elevation

Depth comes from 1px low-opacity borders, subtle radial blue bloom, and layered surface shifts rather than conventional drop shadows. Cards use 22px corners, dark fills on dark sections, and crisp white fills on light sections. Pill controls may use a restrained soft shadow only when they represent an active metric or CTA.

## Components

- **Floating Capsule Navigation**: thin bordered dark pill with the wordmark, section links, and a white consultation CTA.
- **Editorial Grid Hero**: oversized multi-line statement paired with a friendly 3D robot and sparse mono metadata.
- **Parameter Console**: dark configuration panel with sliders, percentages, and a bright blue result chip.
- **Product Proof Cards**: paired tall cards with logo-led white headers and deep dark product descriptions.
- **Service Ledger**: numbered horizontal rows on a light surface with restrained arrow affordances.
- **Portfolio Feature Panel**: cinematic full-width case-study image framed by dark editorial copy.
- **UMKM Tool Shelf**: light product cards with thumbnails, prices, concise feature lists, and pill actions.
- **Mono Section Index**: bracketed chapter labels such as `[ 02 — Jasa untuk UMKM ]`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use exact grid alignment, generous margins, and strong left-edge hierarchy.
- Keep blue scarce and meaningful: one highlighted phrase, control, or result per frame.
- Pair large General Sans statements with small tracked JetBrains Mono metadata.
- Use the 3D robot and product modules as friendly evidence of active building.
- Preserve thin borders and alternating dark/light editorial planes.

### Don'ts

- Do not introduce purple, rainbow gradients, or generic neon-tech palettes.
- Do not use blurry card shadows, glossy glassmorphism, or excessive glow.
- Do not crowd frames with full webpage screenshots or tiny web-scale text.
- Do not replace the official Parameter wordmark with typed imitation.
- Do not make every element rounded; reserve pills for controls and status.
