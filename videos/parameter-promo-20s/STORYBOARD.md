## Video direction

- **Palette system:** Use `frame.md` verbatim: `paper` `#0E0F13` as the permanent ground, `ink` `#F6F7F9` as the primary content color, and `paper-2` / `ink-soft` `#2D6BF5` as the only accent. Every frame keeps the brand-remixed graph grid, top/bottom hairlines, and restrained mono page chrome.
- **Type system:** General Sans display/body roles and JetBrains Mono chrome only. Hierarchy comes from scale, position, and contrast—not extra font weights or invented colors.
- **Motion grammar:** Smooth long-tail settles; every entrance uses a seek-safe explicit from-state. Reveal each phrase, card, and proof asset when the Indonesian voiceover names it, carrying meaningful reveals through the back half of each frame. Internal seams use velocity-matched cuts; holds are still, with only subtle low-amplitude jitter when needed.
- **Rhythm:** Frame 1 is terse and typographic; Frame 2 accumulates outcomes; Frame 3 is the densest evidence beat; Frame 4 deliberately slows into a centered held CTA. Frames 1 and 4 receive the longest clean reads.
- **Caption keep-out:** Keep all load-bearing content above the bottom 17% caption band.
- **Never:** no purple/rainbow gradients, rounded glass cards, generic AI bokeh, repeated bounce, lazy breathing, back-half camera drift, front-loaded slideshow animation, or independent screensaver motion.
